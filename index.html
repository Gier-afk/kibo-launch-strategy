HOW WE MAKE IT

We don't need actors or real filming. We use free stock videos (Pexels), simple Canva slides, and simulated app screens so we can produce videos fast while waiting for app store approval.

Build a library of posts ready to publish. We focus on 3 main hooks: calling strangers late at night, funny language mistakes, and quick voice calls vs. dry texting.
